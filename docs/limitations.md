# Limitations and threats to validity

1. **Population and context.** UCI HAR contains 30 volunteers, aged 19–48, performing six scripted activities with one waist-mounted Samsung Galaxy S II. It does not represent the full range of ages, devices, body placements, environments, transitions, or free-living behavior. Results should not be generalized directly to clinical or commercial deployments.
2. **Windowing and overlap.** The original observations are filtered, segmented 128-sample windows with 50% overlap, not independent continuous recordings. Adjacent windows are correlated. Grouping participants prevents person overlap across validation splits, but windows from a participant and activity share protocol conditions.
3. **Official partition size.** The official test partition contains nine participants. It provides a useful subject-independent comparison but an estimate across a limited set of people; no confidence interval over a large population is available here.
4. **Representation scope.** Custom spectral bands use fixed edges chosen for a 50 Hz sensor and 128-sample windows. Their features are not directly portable to other sampling rates without reconfiguration. The custom descriptor implementation uses nine available axes; it does not reproduce every transform or every derived quantity in the official 561-feature schema.
5. **Model search budget.** Classical tuning explores a small, declared candidate grid; full selector comparisons use three participant-group folds and an intentionally limited MI/PCA dimension grid to fit local runtime constraints. These are not exhaustive searches. Smoke mode has smaller grids and fewer CNN epochs and must not be interpreted as the full experiment.
6. **CNN architecture and optimization.** A compact CNN is trained on CPU and early stopping selects its best of ten epochs on one group-aware training validation partition. The experiment does not claim that this architecture or budget is globally optimal. CNN fit and inference timings depend on the CPU, framework and threading environment.
7. **Importance diagnostics.** Random forest impurity scores can favor certain feature types. Permutation importance is restricted to the top 40 mutual-information-ranked variables and a training-only group-held-out fold. CNN occlusion saliency masks fixed 16-sample segments with standardized zeros and is not causal evidence.
8. **Sensor information.** The provided inertial files are the benchmark's preprocessed/windowed signals; the original collection's filtering and sensor fusion cannot be reconstructed from these signals alone. External validation on new devices or free-living data was not performed.
9. **Performance is benchmark-specific.** The reported UCI test scores measure the official protocol and are not evidence of online streaming latency, battery cost, robustness to missing sensors, or performance after distribution shift.
